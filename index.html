From yarn to your window

Four steps, one belt, zero shortcuts.

Every HRGD piece passes through the same small cluster of weavers northwest of Rohtak — a process that hasn't changed much in the way it's run, only in how carefully we track it.

01 — Sourcing

Raw cotton yarn

Bought directly from ginning units, graded by staple length before it ever touches a dye vat.

02 — Dyeing

Natural & low-impact dyes

Indigo, madder root, and turmeric-based dyes, small-batch, air dried in open yards.

03 — Weaving

Handloom, pit and frame

Woven on traditional looms by artisan families — each bolt carries small, deliberate irregularities.

04 — Finishing

Wash, hem, inspect

Stone-washed for softness, hand-hemmed, and checked thread by thread before it ships.
